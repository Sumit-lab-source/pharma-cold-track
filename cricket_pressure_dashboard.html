📐 Analysis Methodology

Multiplicative Leverage Index Framework

Our proprietary pressure quantification system evaluates every ball across 867 matches using a four-factor multiplicative model, normalized to a 0-100 scale.

1
State Enrichment
Calculate CRR, RRR, wickets/balls remaining per delivery. Phase detection (Powerplay/Middle/Death) for T20 & ODI.
2
Leverage Index (LI)
LI = RRR_factor × Wicket_factor × Phase_factor × Closeness_factor. Format-specific weights applied.
3
Pressure Scoring
PressureScore = min(LI × 20, 100). Bands: Low (0-25), Medium (25-50), High (50-75), Extreme (75-100).
4
Performance Delta
Delta = (Pressure_Metric - Baseline) / Baseline. Higher-better for SR, lower-better for Economy.
5
Clutch Index
Weighted sum: Extreme(0.4) + High(0.3) + Medium(0.2) + Low(0.1) × Volume_Factor.
6
Consistency Score
exp(-CV) where CV = weighted_std/weighted_mean across pressure bands. 1.0 = perfectly consistent.
Final Composite Score
CompositeScore = 0.7 × ClutchIndex + 0.3 × ConsistencyScore
Weights reflect that clutch performance (70%) matters more than mere consistency (30%) for high-pressure situations.
